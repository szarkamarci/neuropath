## Brand & Style

This design system translates cognitive healthcare and neuro-diagnostic technology into a calm, profoundly reassuring visual environment. It bridges clinical rigor with compassionate, human-centered digital care. The target audience comprises clinical neurologists, primary care providers, triaging staff, patients, and family caregivers who require immediate clarity in moments of cognitive concern.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with Soft Humanist Precision**. It blends clean geometric structure, clinical typography, generous negative space, and gentle rounded containers with warm paper-like foundational tones. Rather than cold, sterile hospital software, the visual atmosphere balances medical authority with restorative reassurance.

### Emotional Targets
- **Trustworthy & Evidence-Informed:** Anchored by authoritative deep navy structural elements.
- **Empathetic & Accessible:** Calming soft sky and warm cream underlays that alleviate clinical anxiety.
- **Progressive & Intelligent:** Bright teal/cyan accents that indicate dynamic computational workflows, early triage milestones, and affirmative outcomes.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative medical dark tones with therapeutic atmosphere layers:

- **Primary (`#0B1D3A` - Deep Navy):** The structural anchor. Used for primary headlines, logos, high-emphasis icons, and maximum-contrast dark UI modules (e.g., hero sidebars, primary executive badges).
- **Secondary (`#1BA7A8` - Vibrant Teal/Cyan):** The pathway and action token. Signals actionable triage statuses, key CTA buttons, active state indicators, interactive graph nodes, and emphasis wordmarks.
- **Tertiary (`#4C7DBA` - Slate Blue):** Secondary interactive accents, focused state rings, progress tracking strokes, informational badges, and secondary action buttons.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Cream):** Base atmospheric background for marketing surfaces, editorial views, and high-empathy reading zones to soften screen glare.
  - **Neutral Surface (`#F1F3F6` - Neutral Slate):** Base application dashboard canvas, structural section dividing background, and neutral card borders.
  - **Soft Sky Accent (`#E6F2F2`):** Primary tint for status pill backgrounds, metric badge fills, cognitive score wrappers, and subtle hero glow fields.
- **Semantic Signals:**
  - *Success / Affirmative:* `#1BA7A8`
  - *Attention / At Risk:* `#E26D5C`
  - *Neutral Review:* `#4C7DBA`
  - *Surface Containers:* `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

The type scale is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, matching the visual brand's friendly geometric anatomy, open counters, and high legibility across clinical dashboards and consumer mobile experiences.

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#0B1D3A`) with weights 600 and 700. Emphasize key diagnostic terms or concluding statements through the secondary teal accent (`#1BA7A8`).
- **Body Text:** Neutralized with a softened dark slate value (`#334155`) over warm or neutral surfaces to preserve reading comfort during cognitive screening tasks.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp 600 weight used for section headers, data metadata cards, and navigation items.

## Layout & Spacing

The system leverages a strict **8pt / 0.5rem modular spatial system** combined with an asymmetric fluid grid.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`gutter`) columns and 48px (`margin`) outer padding. Contained max-width of 1280px for standard applications and 1440px for full-scale dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Multi-column metric summary tiles collapse from 4-across to a 2x2 grid or a horizontal snap carousel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using clean ambient light, tonal layering, and fine boundary lines.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#FAF7F2` or `#F1F3F6`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Tile Default):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#F1F3F6`, bordered by a subtle 1px border (`rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.06)`), combined with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Used for hovered cards and popovers: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** Used for cognitive assessment dialogs and triage panels: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.16)`.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Backgrounds employ large, soft radial gradients in Soft Sky (`#E6F2F2`) to gently frame key hero visuals and pathway graphics without introducing hard boundaries.

## Shapes

The geometry uses **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature to preserve medical precision while presenting an approachable, human-centered touchpoint:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, tooltips, inline alerts, and small interactive chips.
- **Medium Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Metric dashboard cards, patient triage blocks, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Modal dialogs, application hero cards, and triage overview containers.
- **Pill (9999px):** Primary call-to-action buttons, metric status indicators, and notification badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#1BA7A8`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px`, vertical padding `0.75rem`, horizontal padding `1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#158788`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent background, border `1.5px solid #1BA7A8`, text `#1BA7A8`, border-radius `9999px`. Hover fills with `#E6F2F2`.
- **Tertiary / Neutral Action:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0B1D3A`. Hover adds Level 1 shadow.

### Metric Overview Cards (Badge Metrics)
- Constructed with a white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 12px radius, and a 1px border in `#F1F3F6`.
- Features an upper icon container (36x36px) filled with `#E6F2F2` housing a line-art diagnostic icon in `#1BA7A8` or `#4C7DBA`.
- Metric numbers are set in `metric-number` (`#0B1D3A`), with a secondary label beneath in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Navigation Headers
- Fixed, clean 72px height navbar with high-clarity backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- Border bottom: `1px solid rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.06)`.
- Navigation items styled with `label-lg` in `#0B1D3A`, transitioning to `#1BA7A8` on active or hover states. Contains a right-aligned primary CTA button.

### Form Inputs
- 48px height, 8px border radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state rings with `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 167, 168, 0.2)` and border `#1BA7A8`.

### Hero Banner
- Asymmetric 2-column layout. Left column houses high-impact typography with contrasting cyan highlights, followed by an explanatory lead paragraph in `#334155` and a button group. Right column houses dynamic anatomical and pathway illustrations framed with ambient `#E6F2F2` blurs.
